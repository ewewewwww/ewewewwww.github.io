AMAPLA Uploader

A personal desktop tool used by the creator of the YouTube channel AMAPLA (gunpla & model kit painting) to share their own short videos to their own Instagram, Facebook Page and TikTok accounts.

The tool runs only on the creator's own computer. It is not offered to other users.
